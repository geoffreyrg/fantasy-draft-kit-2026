Optimal Draft Strategy in 2026
What's the optimal strategy in 2026 drafts?
Every strategy can, should, and will work this year. If I'm doing my job right, you should be
able to win your league even if you fell asleep after Round 8.
In a vacuum and as a general rule, you’re probably going to want to draft at least two RBs by
the end of Round 3. You’re going to want to wait at the QB position. And you’re going to want
to load up on WRs in the middle rounds of your draft. Blah, blah, blah.
But again — every strategy will work. You should be able to win whether you start RB-RB or
go full Zero-RB and don't take an RB until Round 5 (though I think the latter strategy is ill-
advised). I typically caution against an early-round QB or TE, but if current ADP holds, that
could actually be optimal given how much late-round value there is this year.
So feel comforted knowing you've got some wiggle room here. You have permission to be a
homer and go after some players on your favorite team, or reach a bit too high on a defense
because you're too lazy to stream it. Really, there's really no wrong answer this year.
“Okay, yeah, yeah, yeah. But what *exactly* do you think is optimal?”
In a vacuum and broadly speaking, I want…

Kicker / Defense
Yeah…
In most leagues, I’m typically not even drafting a kicker or a defense. Instead, I’ll be drafting
high-upside flex-eligible players. And then, right before Week 1, I’ll drop the two worst players
on my team, and pick up whichever K and DEF I like best on waivers.
If that isn’t an option, or if I’m drafting particularly late in the offseason, I’ll always draft a kicker
and then a defense (in that order) with my last two picks.
Who am I taking? It really doesn’t matter. Usually, whichever K ranks highest in our

ADP is generally really bad at predicting who the top kickers and defenses are going to be in
a given season. K and DEF are also extremely deep, very replaceable, and not worth much at
all in terms of winning your league.
