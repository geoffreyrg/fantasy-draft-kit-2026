### Key & Legend

* 💥 **Exodia / League-Winner:** High power-law upside player meeting core metric thresholds (e.g. Big 3 RB traits, 1D/RR $\ge 0.110$, alpha TPRR).
* 🎯 **Target / Value / Sleeper:** Core structural builds, positive regression candidates, or late-round lottery tickets.
* 🚫 **Fade / Avoid / Overvalue / Dirty 30:** Market traps, injury landmines, poor metric profiles, or touch-split risks.
* ⚔️ **DISAGREEMENT:** Explicit divergence in valuation or classification between Fantasy Points and Joel Smyth.
* 💰 **Auction Value:** Projected baseline auction price for a standard $200 salary cap format.
