## 4. 📐 The Definitive 2026 Draft Blueprint Rules

1. **Quarterback**: Go as cheap as possible. Stream or take the **"Mobile Middle Class"** under a McShanahan playcaller (**Trevor Lawrence, Justin Herbert, Kyler Murray, Malik Willis**).
2. **Running Back**: Secure **2 RBs in the first 3 rounds** (Gibbs, Bijan, JT, CMC, Chase Brown, Ashton Jeanty, Kenneth Walker, Omarion Hampton). Draft **David Montgomery** in Round 5 and **Jonathon Brooks** in Rounds 8-10. Avoid the "Average Bellcow Dead Zone" (Kyren, Etienne, Javonte).
3. **Wide Receiver**: Target **4-5 WRs before Round 10**. Exploit mispriced 1D/RR alphas (**Puka, JSN, London, Waddle, Watson, Evans, Burden, Washington, Downs, Godwin**).
4. **Tight End**: Wait for **Tucker Kraft (ESPN)** or **George Kittle (Yahoo/Sleeper)** and pair with **Dallas Goedert / Isaiah Likely**.
5. **K / DST**: Do not draft until final 2 rounds, or leave open for preseason contingent RB stashes (**Jake Bates** = Exodia Kicker; **Jacksonville Jaguars** = Exodia DST).
